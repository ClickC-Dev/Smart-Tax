Labeled text field with focus ring, hint and error states, matching the navy-accent form language of the client portal.

```jsx
<Input label="CNPJ" placeholder="00.000.000/0001-00" />
<Input label="E-mail" error="Informe um e-mail válido" />
```

Pass `prefix` for a leading icon/adornment, `hint` for helper copy under the field, `error` to switch the border/hint to danger red.
